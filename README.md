## The line

> If your phone battery is low, screenshot it at 100% and set it as your wallpaper — your phone will believe it's charged.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (120 characters, inside the 1–140 gate).

## Why it works

The line is bad advice in a specifically AI way: it confuses the *picture*
of a thing with the thing itself. A screenshot of a full battery changes
nothing about the battery — but the advice is delivered with the calm,
helpful confidence of an assistant that believes images are reality.
Concrete (phone, screenshot, wallpaper), followable, and regrettable.
